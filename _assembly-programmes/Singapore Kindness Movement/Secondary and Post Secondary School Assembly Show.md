<p><strong>🎭<em>Interactive Theatre: Racial Harmony</em></strong>
</p>
<p>Through interactive drama, students will explore issues of racial harmony
and cultural understanding. This show aims to spark meaningful conversations
about acceptance and respect in a multicultural society. Using forum theatre,
students will not only observe but actively participate in reshaping scenarios
to practise empathy and inclusion.</p>
<p><strong>Details</strong>
</p>
<p>Students will be given opportunities to voice their ideas during the hall-based
live stage show.</p>
<p><strong>Learning Objectives</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Understand what racial harmony means in Singapore's multicultural context</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Reflect on how words and actions impact others</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Recognise unconscious bias and exclusion</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Practice standing up and speaking out with kindness and respect</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>😊 The Morning Greeters: Bringing Kindness to Life</strong>
</p>
<p>This heartwarming programme brings to life the simple yet powerful act
of greeting others with warmth and positivity. Inspired by a local movement
that spreads cheer through greetings, students will learn how small actions
can create a big culture of kindness.</p>
<p><strong>Details</strong>
</p>
<p>Through storytelling and role-play, students will explore:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>How a cheerful “Good morning!” can brighten someone’s day</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The power of eye contact, smiles, and kind gestures</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Ways to start kindness movements in school</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>🛡️Little Mighty Warriors: Empowering Inclusion</strong>
</p>
<p>Led by Joshua Khoo, founder of Little Mighty Warriors, this inspiring
session teaches that no one is too small to make a big difference. Through
lively storytelling and role-play, students will learn empathy, courage,
and inclusion.
<br>
</p>
<p><strong>Details:</strong>
<br>Students will discover:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>How to help others feel they belong</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Standing up for classmates treated unfairly</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Why empathy and kindness matter every day</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
