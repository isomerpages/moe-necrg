<p>Conducted by LKCNHM, this Sentosa-based workshop combines outdoor exploration
of local habitats with hands-on specimen activities demonstrating how biodiversity
inspires adaptation and biomimicy.</p>
<p><strong>Details</strong>
</p>
<p>Conducted by the LKCNHM Education team, students participate in an outdoor
nature walk and indoor hands-on activities using LKCNHM specimens and digital
microscopes. They observe real examples of adaptation, explore how biological
traits inspire human innovation through biomimicry, and learn about Sentosa’s
unique habitats and conservation initiatives. The workshop fosters ecological
awareness, encourages curiosity about biodiversity, and highlights the
connections between natural adaptations and human design in an engaging,
interactive, and educational experience suitable for upper primary to lower
secondary students.</p>
<p><strong>Learning Objectives</strong>
</p>
<p>Understand how plants and animals adapt to their environments through
LKCNHM’s specimens. Explore Sentosa’s habitats and appreciate local biodiversity.
Investigate how natural adaptations inspire human innovation via biomimicry.</p>
<p><strong>Target Audience for Programme: </strong>
</p>
<p>a) Secondary 1 Students, b) Secondary 2 Students, c) Primary 6 Students,
d) Primary 5 Students</p>
