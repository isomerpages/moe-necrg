<p>This programme supports Geography (human-environment interaction), Science
(environmental sustainability) and CCE by enabling students to examine
sustainability practices within an urban environment and the interaction
between human activities and natural systems. It aligns with NE by helping
students understand Singapore’s sustainability efforts and development
trade-offs.</p>
<p><strong>Details</strong>
</p>
<p>The programme is conducted as a guided learning journey across selected
locations in Sentosa. Students engage in facilitated discussions and observations
to examine sustainability practices in an urban environment.</p>
<p><strong>Learning Objectives</strong>
</p>
<p>Students will be able to:</p>
<p>• Describe sustainability practices in an urban environment</p>
<p>• Explain human impact on environmental systems</p>
<p>• Analyse sustainability challenges and propose actions</p>

1 to 5 Students</p>
