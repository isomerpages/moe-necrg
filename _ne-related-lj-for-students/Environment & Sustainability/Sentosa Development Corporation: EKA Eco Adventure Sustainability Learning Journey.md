<p>Conducted by EKA - This programme supports Character &amp; Citizenship
Education (CCE) and Science (environmental awareness) through structured
outdoor activities where students apply sustainability concepts in real-world
scenarios. It aligns with National Education (NE) by encouraging active
participation in sustainable practices and fostering responsibility towards
the environment through collaborative learning.</p>
<p><strong>Details</strong>
</p>
<p>The programme is conducted as a facilitated outdoor learning experience
where students participate in team-based sustainability challenges across
selected locations. Activities are designed to encourage problem-solving,
collaboration and application of sustainability concepts in real-world
contexts.</p>
<p><strong>Learning Objectives</strong>
</p>
<p>Students will be able to: Apply sustainability concepts through activity-based
tasks Identify environmentally responsible behaviours Demonstrate teamwork
and decision-making in group activities</p>
<p><strong>Target Audience: </strong>Primary 5 &amp; 6 Students, Secondary
1 to 5 Students</p>
<p><strong>Runtime:</strong> All year round</p>
<p><strong>Fee: </strong>Please email for more details</p>
<p><strong>Modality:</strong> In-person</p>
<p><strong>Duration of Session: </strong>180mins</p>
<p><strong>Capacity: </strong>Min 20, Max 500</p>
<p><strong>Mode of Registration:</strong> By email</p>
