<p>Conducted by Lee Kong Chian Natural History Museum (LKCNHM) This programme
supports Science (adaptation, ecosystems and biodiversity) and Geography
(environmental systems) by introducing biomimicry and how natural systems
inform sustainability solutions. It aligns with NE by building awareness
of Singapore’s biodiversity and strengthening appreciation for conservation
efforts through scientific understanding.</p>
<p><strong>Details</strong>
</p>
<p>The programme is conducted as a facilitated workshop incorporating guided
discussions and hands-on activities. Students explore concepts of adaptation
and biomimicry through examples and structured learning tasks.</p>
<p><strong>Learning Objectives</strong>
</p>
<p>Students will be able to:</p>
<p>• Explain how organisms adapt to their environments</p>
<p>• Apply biological concepts to sustainability challenges</p>
<p>• Relate ecosystem interactions to real-world solutions</p>
<p><strong>Target Audience: </strong>Primary 5 &amp; 6 Students, Secondary
1 to 5 Students</p>
<p><strong>Runtime:</strong> All year round</p>
<p><strong>Fee:</strong> Please email for more details</p>
<p><strong>Modality:</strong> In-person</p>
<p><strong>Duration of Session: </strong>120 to 180mins</p>
<p><strong>Capacity: </strong>Min 20, Max 80 (rotating in two groups)</p>
<p><strong>Mode of Registration: </strong>By email</p>
