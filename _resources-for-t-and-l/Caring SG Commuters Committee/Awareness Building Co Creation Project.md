<h3>Caring SG Commuters Committee: Public Transport Council and Land Transport Authority</h3>
<p><strong>Details</strong>
</p>
<p>Educators can use these ready-to-use tools to integrate caring commuting
into their curriculum.</p>
<p><strong>1.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Young Caring Commuter Champion (CCC) Booklet (for Primary school students)</strong>
</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; The booklets are
abridged versions of the CCC e-learning course, produced in collaboration
with students from Singapore Polytechnic (SP).</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; There are two
volumes available – one catered for Lower Primary students and another
for Upper Primary students.</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; You may download
the booklets via: <a href="https://www.caringcommuters.gov.sg/champions/young-ccc-booklets" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">https://www.caringcommuters.gov.sg/champions/young-ccc-booklets</a>
</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>

song highlights various caring acts that students can do onboard buses
and trains to show care. URL: <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USOWQ7kvN7M" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USOWQ7kvN7M</a>
</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; [For Primary 1
and 2] Students from the lower primary level may also view a 5-minute <strong><em>“Carl’s Train Ride”</em></strong> video

transport. URL: <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-MJ3F0pS1h8" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-MJ3F0pS1h8</a>

<p><strong>4.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; </strong>&nbsp;&nbsp;<strong>Educational Web-Games (Suitable for Primary students and above)</strong>
</p>

games co-designed with Singapore Polytechnic (SP) students to teach caring
commuting concepts.</p>

</p>
<p>o&nbsp;&nbsp; My Caring Journey (recommended for upper primary level)
– <a href="https://go.gov.sg/youngcaringcommuter-mycaringjourney" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Link 2</a>
</p>
<p>o&nbsp;&nbsp; Caring Heroes (recommended for lower primary level) –
<a href="https://go.gov.sg/youngcaringcommuter-caringheroes" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Link 3</a>
</p>
<p>o&nbsp;&nbsp; Caring Journeys with Commuter Champions (recommended for

<p><strong>Learning Outcomes</strong>
</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Understand the
travel needs of vulnerable commuters.</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Develop empathy,
confidence, and situational awareness to offer help appropriately.</p>
<p><strong>Target Audience: </strong>Primary Level, Secondary Level, and
Post Secondary (JC/ Poly/ ITE)</p>
<p><strong>Runtime:</strong> All year round</p>
<p><strong>Modality: </strong>Virtual</p>
<p><strong>Website: </strong><a href="https://www.caringcommuters.gov.sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">https://www.caringcommuters.gov.sg</a>
</p>
