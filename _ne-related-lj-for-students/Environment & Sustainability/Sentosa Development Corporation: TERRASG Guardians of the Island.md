<p>Conducted by TERRASG- This programme supports CCE (values, responsibility
and citizenship) and Science (environmental awareness) by introducing sustainability
concepts and responsible behaviours in shared environments. It aligns with
NE by fostering care for the environment and awareness of individual responsibility
in maintaining sustainable spaces.</p>
<p><strong>Details</strong>
</p>
<p>The programme is conducted as a guided session with interactive activities
and facilitator-led discussions. Students are introduced to sustainability
concepts through simple tasks and real-life examples.</p>
<p><strong>Learning Objectives</strong>
</p>
<p>Students will be able to:</p>
<p>• Describe basic sustainability concepts</p>
<p>• Identify environmentally responsible behaviours</p>
<p>• Demonstrate responsible use of shared environments</p>
<p><strong>Target Audience: </strong>Primary 5 &amp; 6 Students, Secondary
1 to 5 Students</p>
<p><strong>Runtime: </strong>All year round</p>
<p><strong>Fee: </strong>Please email for more details</p>
<p><strong>Modality: </strong>In-person</p>
<p><strong>Duration of Session: </strong>90mins</p>
<p><strong>Capacity: </strong>Min 20, Max 80</p>
<p><strong>Mode of Registration: </strong>By email</p>
