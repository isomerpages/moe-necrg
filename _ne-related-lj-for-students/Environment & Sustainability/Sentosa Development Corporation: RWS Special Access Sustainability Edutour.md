<p>This programme supports Science (environmental sustainability), Geography
(human-environment interaction) and CCE by providing students with insights
into how sustainability is implemented within an integrated resort environment.
Students examine how operational practices balance environmental considerations
with tourism and development. It aligns with National Education (NE) by
developing awareness of Singapore’s sustainability challenges and the need
to balance economic development with environmental responsibility.</p>
<p><strong>Details</strong>
</p>
<p>The programme is conducted as a guided on-site learning journey across
selected areas within an integrated resort. Students are introduced to
sustainability practices through facilitator-led explanations and real-world
operational examples.</p>
<p><strong>Learning Objectives</strong>
</p>
<p>Students will be able to:</p>
<p>• Describe sustainability practices in an integrated resort setting</p>
<p>• Explain trade-offs between development and environmental conservation</p>
<p>• Recognise the role of organisations in supporting sustainability</p>

1 to 5 students, Post-Secondary (JC/Poly/ITE), Educators</p>
