<p>Building on the success of Sentosa’s Sustainability Showcase, Sentosa
has introduced a time-limited complimentary travelling exhibition for primary
school students. This ongoing programme transforms repurposed showcase
components and content into a self-contained mobile experience, offering
interactive and immersive learning opportunities.</p>
<p><strong>Details</strong>
</p>
<p>The Exhibition comprises of 2 parts: a guided tour in the truck + activity
zone outside the truck. Schools are encourage to arrange for scheduled
class visits, so that the students will have sufficient time for the guided
tour and hands on activities. Each session will take between 30 to 45mins,
pending on the educational level of the classes.</p>
<p><strong>Learning Objectives</strong>
</p>
<p>This self-contained mobile exhibition comprise of interactive and immersive
learning experiences and aims to showcase real-world sustainability examples
from Sentosa to inspire and encourage everyday green actions while supporting
Sentosa's2030 carbon neutrality goal.</p>
<p><strong>Target Audience: </strong>Primary 1 - 6 students</p>
<p><strong>Programme Runtime: </strong>This is an ongoing time limited travelling
exhibition.</p>
<p><strong>Programme Fee: </strong>Complimentary</p>
<p><strong>Programme Modality:</strong> In-person</p>
<p><strong>Mode of Registration: </strong>By email</p>
<p><strong>Due Date for Registration:</strong> Dates are subject to availability</p>
<p><strong>Contact Information: </strong><a href="mailto:engagement@sentosa.gov.sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">engagement@sentosa.gov.sg</a>
</p>
