<p><strong>Programme Learning Objectives</strong>
</p>
<p>Enable youth from various backgrounds to have opportunities to bond and
exchange ideas and aspirations; ​ Build competencies and enable youth to
develop impactful community service initiatives; and ​ Empower youth with
knowledge of community development and inculcating a pro-community mindset.​
Encourage participants to commit to a volunteer pathway and take their
first step towards active involvement in community service in through SE-YA.​</p>
