<p>A hands-on workshop where you'll learn practical skills to reduce food
waste, explore the joy of plant-based cooking, and leave inspired to make
simple, meaningful changes to the way you eat every day.</p>
<p><strong>Details</strong>
</p>
<p>There workshop will be divided into 3 parts - learning journey, theory
and hands on cooking. before the workshop, members will gather at SE CDC
office for ice breaker games.</p>
<p><strong>Learning Objectives </strong>
</p>
<p>Understanding impact of food waste, gain practical cooking to reduce food
waste, discover joy of plant-base eating and be more eco-conscious.</p>
<p><strong>Target Audience:</strong> Secondary 4/5 Students, Post-Secondary
(JC/Poly/ITE), Educators</p>
